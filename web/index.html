Yellow labels: press SHIFT first, or hold the key. Purple labels: press ALPHA first. Tap an example to load it.

Top keys

MODE
Civil engineering calculators (BRICKWORK, CONCRETE), UNITS, FEET-INCHES, EQN, MATRIX, VECTOR, STAT, DISTR, TABLE, plus angle unit, Fix/Sci decimals and fraction or decimal answers.
2nd
List of every function. Tap one to type it.
▲ ▼ ◀ ▶
▲▼ step through past calculations, ◀▶ move the cursor
CALC
Works out an expression for values you enter, e.g. 2X+Y. Also stores Y=2X+3 into Y.
SOLVE
SHIFT CALC. Finds X in an equation (ALPHA CALC types =), e.g. X²−5X+6=0
∫dx · d/dx
∫(f, a, b) is the area under f(X). d/dx(f, a) is the slope at X = a. ALPHA ∫dx types ;
x⁻¹ · x! · ><=
1 ÷ x, factorial, and comparison signs (> < ≥ ≤ ≠). A comparison answers True or False.
logₓy · Σ · Π
Log to any base logₓ(base, value). Σ(f, a, b) sums f(X) and Π(f, a, b) multiplies f(X) for X = a to b.

Number keys

CONST · CONV · SI
Engineering and science constants, the unit converter, and SI prefixes (k = ᴇ3, M = ᴇ6, m = ᴇ-3 …)

lim(f, a): the value f(X) approaches as X → a (a can be ∞)
MATRIX · VECTOR
Edit matrices A, B (up to 4×4): +, −, ×, determinant, inverse, transpose, solve A·x = B. Vectors: dot, cross, length, angle.

A table of values for f(X) and g(X) from a start to an end value
STAT
Mean, standard deviation, quartiles, sums. 2-VAR also gives the line of best fit y = a + bx and r.

nPr nCr GCD LCM

Pol · Rec
