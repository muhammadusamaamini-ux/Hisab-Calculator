HELP · what every key does

Yellow labels: press SHIFT first, or hold the key. Purple labels: press ALPHA first. Tap an example to load it.

Top keys

MODE
Setup: COMP or CMPLX, angle unit, Fix/Sci decimals, fraction or decimal answers. Also opens EQN, MATRIX, VECTOR, STAT, DISTR and TABLE.
2nd
List of every function. Tap one to type it.
▲ ▼ ◀ ▶
▲▼ step through past calculations, ◀▶ move the cursor
CALC
Works out an expression for values you enter, e.g. 2X+Y. Also stores Y=2X+3 into Y.
SOLVE
SHIFT CALC. Finds X in an equation (ALPHA CALC types =), e.g. X²−5X+6=0
∫dx · d/dx
∫(f, a, b) is the area under f(X). d/dx(f, a) is the slope at X = a. ALPHA ∫dx types ;
x⁻¹ · x! · ><=
1 ÷ x, factorial, and comparison signs (> < ≥ ≤ ≠). A comparison answers True or False.
logₓy · Σ · Π
Log to any base logₓ(base, value). Σ(f, a, b) sums f(X) and Π(f, a, b) multiplies f(X) for X = a to b.

Function keys

x/y · x y/z
Fractions: 3 x/y 4 is 3⁄4. For 2 3⁄4 press SHIFT x/y (a space) between 2 and 3⁄4. ALPHA gives ÷R (quotient and remainder).
√x · ∛x · mod
Square root, cube root, mod(a, b) remainder
x² · x³ · 0.‾3

Logs and their inverses

°′″ · FACT

sin cos tan

Number keys

CONST · CONV · SI
Engineering and science constants, the unit converter, and SI prefixes (k = ᴇ3, M = ᴇ6, m = ᴇ-3 …)
Limit · ∞
lim(f, a): the value f(X) approaches as X → a (a can be ∞)
MATRIX · VECTOR
Edit matrices A, B (up to 4×4): +, −, ×, determinant, inverse, transpose, solve A·x = B. Vectors: dot, cross, length, angle.
FUNC
A table of values for f(X) and g(X) from a start to an end value
STAT
Mean, standard deviation, quartiles, sums. 2-VAR also gives the line of best fit y = a + bx and r.
CMPLX
Turns complex numbers on, sets a+bi or r∠θ answers, and has Arg, Conj, Real, Imag
DISTR
Normal, inverse normal, binomial and Poisson probabilities
nPr nCr GCD LCM
Permutations, combinations, greatest common divisor, least common multiple
Pol · Rec
Pol(Δx, Δy) gives distance and angle. Rec(distance, angle) gives Δx and Δy (surveying). Answers go into X and Y.
Ceil · Floor
Round up or down to a whole number
COPY · PASTE
Copy the answer, then paste it into a later calculation
Ran# · RanInt
Random number from 0 to 1, or a random whole number RanInt(a, b)

EQN · equations

How to use feet and inches

65 ft 2 3/8
Press 6 5, ft, 2, then tap 3/8 in the fraction row
65 ft
Just press 6 5. A number on its own is feet.
7 1/2 in
Press 7, then tap 1/2. A number followed by a fraction is inches.
9 in
Press 9, in
1/16 fractions
Tap “16ths” at the end of the fraction row
+ − × ÷ =
Work like a site calculator, left to right. Every step stays on the tape above.
× 3, ÷ 4
After × or ÷ a plain number is a multiplier. Press ft or in after it to multiply two lengths (area) or three (volume).
20 ÷ 2 6
Length ÷ length tells you how many pieces fit (here 8)
⌫
Deletes the last digit. On an empty line it brings back the last entry to fix it.
Type
Use the phone keyboard, Revit style: 65 2 3/8 means 65 ft 2 3/8 in
